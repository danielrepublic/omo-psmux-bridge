(/home/daniel/omo-psmux-bridge-wt/windows-parity/scripts/windows/):

  The $BinDir default was moved from the param block to the body of the

    1. $BinDir parameter (if provided)
    2. $PSScriptRoot (if non-empty)

  [System.IO.Path]::GetFullPath($BinDir).TrimEnd('\').
